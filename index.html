From our kitchen

Home-cooked food, the way we eat it

Everything we serve is cooked fresh in our own kitchen by our family, with vegetables we grow ourselves whenever the season allows.

Mornings start with a hot breakfast and a pot of tea in the dining room, with the mountains outside the window. In the evening, come back to warming Ladakhi dishes like thenthuk and mok-thuk, steaming momos and skyu, or simple Indian home food of dal, rice, sabzi and fresh roti.

Try a cup of salty butter tea the way Ladakhi families drink it, or stay with sweet masala chai. Tell us about any diet or allergy and we will cook for it, and on your first day at altitude we keep the food light and gentle.

Cooked with care, served like family.

Experiences with us

See Leh the way we know it

Alongside your stay, our family offers a few small outings around Leh. There is nothing to book in advance. Just let us know whenever you feel like joining one.

Sunrise

Sunrise walk to Shanti Stupa

Set off in the cool before dawn and climb to Shanti Stupa as the first light reaches the peaks around Leh. We walk at an easy pace, then share something warm to drink while the town wakes up below.

₹500 per person, refreshments included
Sunset

Sunset walk to Tsemo

Wind up through the old town lanes to Tsemo as the evening turns gold. From the top you see the whole valley, Leh Palace and the Stok range, and we stop for refreshments on the way back down.

₹500 per person, refreshments included
Half day

Picnic by the stream in Gangles

Spend a slow afternoon in the village of Gangles, beside a mountain stream just outside Leh. We pack the food, the tea and the blankets; you bring your appetite and a book.

₹1,500 per person, everything included
Evening

Cook a Ladakhi meal with the family

Join us in our kitchen to make a home-style Ladakhi meal, from kneading the dough to the last stir of the pot. Then sit down together and eat what you made.

₹800 per person, dinner included
Morning

Leh old town walk

Explore the narrow lanes, old houses and hidden corners of Leh's old town with someone who grew up around them. We share the stories behind the places most visitors walk straight past.

Explore Ladakh

Start in Leh, then go further

A few favourites to plan around. Tap a place to see what to expect and a suggested itinerary. We can help with taxis, bikes and permits for all of them.

Overnight

Pangong Tso

The long high-altitude lake that changes from turquoise to deep blue through the day, reached over the Chang La pass.

Overnight

Tso Moriri

A quiet, deep-blue lake in the Changthang plateau, with nomad camps, wild asses and far fewer visitors than Pangong.

Overnight

Nubra Valley

Cross Khardung La to sand dunes, double-humped camels and the villages of Diskit and Hunder.

Day trip

Monasteries along the Indus

Thiksey, Hemis, Shey and Alchi are built into the hillsides east and west of Leh, each an easy day out by taxi.

Day trip

Rafting on the Indus and Zanskar

Summer rafting trips with local operators, from gentle stretches for beginners to faster water for the more daring.

Day trip or trek

Hemis National Park

High, rugged country that is home to the snow leopard, blue sheep and the Himalayan ibex, with winter treks for wildlife watchers.

    Questions travellers ask us

    How should I handle the altitude?

    Leh sits at about 3,500 metres. Plan to rest for your first day or two, drink plenty of water and avoid hard exercise until you feel well. If you fly in, give yourself a quiet first day before any passes.

    When are you open?

    We welcome guests from April to October, which is the main travel season in Ladakh.

    Do I need permits?

    Leh town needs no permit, but places like Nubra Valley and Pangong Tso do. Tell us your plans and we'll help you arrange them.

    Can you pick me up from the airport?

    Yes. Send us your flight details on WhatsApp and we'll arrange a pickup.

    What is your cancellation policy?

    It depends on the room and how you book. If you book with us directly, ask us on WhatsApp and we'll explain the terms for your dates. On Booking.com or Agoda, the conditions are shown with each room option.

    Can I store my luggage while I travel?

    Yes. Leave your bags with us while you go to Nubra, Pangong or Tso Moriri, and pick them up when you're back.
