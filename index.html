    From our kitchen

    Home-cooked food, the way we eat it

    Everything we serve is cooked fresh in our own kitchen by our family, with vegetables from local farms whenever the season allows.

    Mornings start with a hot breakfast and a pot of tea in the dining room, with the mountains outside the window. In the evening, come back to warming Ladakhi dishes like thenthuk and mok-thuk, steaming momos and skyu, or simple Indian home food of dal, rice, sabzi and fresh roti.

    Try a cup of salty butter tea the way Ladakhi families drink it, or stay with sweet masala chai. Tell us about any diet or allergy and we will cook for it, and on your first day at altitude we keep the food light and gentle.

    Cooked with care, served like family.

      Questions travellers ask us

      How should I handle the altitude?

      Leh sits at about 3,500 metres. Plan to rest for your first day or two, drink plenty of water and avoid hard exercise until you feel well. If you fly in, give yourself a quiet first day before any passes.

      When are you open?

      We welcome guests from April to October, which is the main travel season in Ladakh.

      Do I need permits?

      Leh town needs no permit, but places like Nubra Valley and Pangong Tso do. Tell us your plans and we'll help you arrange them.

      Can you pick me up from the airport?

      Yes. Send us your flight details on WhatsApp and we'll arrange a pickup.
